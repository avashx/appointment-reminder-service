Why a reminder can't go out twice

  1. One row per reminder. UNIQUE (appointment, revision, type) on the reminder table.
  2. One worker per attempt. Claimed with FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED, leased until processing_until, every write fenced by a claim token.
  3. One hand-off per key. The idempotency key is committed to notification_delivery before the provider is called; a retry finds it and sends nothing.
